Research

Causal Forests Under Staggered Enrollment: Heterogeneous Effects of a Statewide Literacy Program

Virtual Learning Lab, University of Florida · 2026 – Ongoing · Honors thesis & UF AI Scholar project

Evaluating which K–5 students benefit most from a statewide literacy program in the Southeastern U.S., using assessment data on 1.4 million students, a staggered difference-in-differences design, and causal forests.

Automating Word Ladder Generation for Digital Blending Drills

Virtual Learning Lab, University of Florida · 2025 – 2026 · Best Research Poster Award, DSI Spring Symposium

Word ladders let early readers practice decoding one sound at a time, but existing digital versions are fixed in content. This project generates them automatically for each lesson so teachers can match practice to what they're actually teaching.

Historical Document Transcription for OCR

KGB Lab, University of Florida · 2026 – Ongoing

I transcribe and annotate handwritten Soviet-era Russian documents from declassified secret police archives; the transcriptions serve as training data for the lab's OCR models.
