Research

Predicting Reading Difficulty Risk in Early Readers

Virtual Learning Lab, University of Florida · 2026-2027

Developing an interpretable machine learning early warning system that uses session level phoneme blending drill performance to identify students at risk of delayed decoding growth. Work covers feature construction from raw drill interactions, at-risk labeling, and training classification models evaluated on AUC and sensitivity.

Automated Word Ladder Generation for Digital Blending Drills

Virtual Learning Lab, University of Florida · 2025-2026

Developed a system to automatically generate word ladders for digital blending drills, ensuring alignment with lesson content and appropriate difficulty for students. Work included algorithm design, implementation, and evaluation of generated ladders.
