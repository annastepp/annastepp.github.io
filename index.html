About

My name is Anna Stepanova, and I study Statistics and Economics, with a minor in Mathematics, at the University of Florida. I am a senior, a UF AI Scholar, and I will graduate in May 2027.

I work in the Virtual Learning Lab with Dr. Walter Leite, where I evaluate a statewide program in Florida. that mails free books to students. Using assessment data on 1.4 million elementary students, I study which students the program helps most. This work is the basis of my honors thesis.

I am applying to PhD programs in statistics and machine learning for Fall 2027.

Research interests

  • Statistical machine learning theory — why methods like neural networks and dimensionality reduction work
  • Representation learning — how models uncover structure in high-dimensional data
  • Applications to social and education data — large, messy administrative datasets and who programs actually help
